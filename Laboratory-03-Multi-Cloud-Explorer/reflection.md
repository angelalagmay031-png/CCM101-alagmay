# 📝 Reflection

The cloud platform that impressed me most was AWS because of its wide range of services and flexibility. AWS, Azure, and GCP are similar because they all provide computing, storage, networking, databases, security, and other cloud services. They allow organizations to use resources without maintaining all physical infrastructure themselves.

Business requirements strongly influence cloud platform selection. A company should consider its existing technology, budget, application requirements, security needs, scalability, and technical skills. For example, an organization heavily dependent on Microsoft technologies may benefit more from Azure, while an AI-focused company may prefer GCP.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant helped me understand that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. The correct choice depends on the problem that needs to be solved and the services required by the organization.

This mission also improved my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio because I organized my research, comparisons, recommendations, screenshots, and reflection into separate Markdown files. I learned that professional cloud documentation should be clear, organized, and supported by evidence.

Overall, this activity helped me understand the similarities and differences between AWS, Azure, and GCP and improved my ability to connect technical cloud services with real business requirements.
